# Figure legends

**Fig. 1 | A harmonized multi-study resource of rhizosphere 16S rRNA gene sequencing data and its calibration framework.** **a** Evidence architecture linking public-data harmonization, eight-summary simulation calibration, four analysis domains and project-panel holdout. **b** Samples per development project (n = 45; 15 maize, 15 rice and 15 wheat projects). **c** Numbers of generated datasets, random seeds or split units used in each analysis layer. **d** Fraction of prespecified checks meeting operational tolerances. Denominators are 80 no-effect DA method-dataset checks (four methods × 20 seeds), 10 core stage-scenario calibration cells, 144 three-panel structural checks (three panels × two generators × three seeds × eight summaries) and 480 held-out structural checks (30 panels × two generators × eight summaries). The benchmark supports comparisons within the evaluated scenarios and tasks, not a universal method ranking. Source data are provided in the analysis package.

**Fig. 2 | Differential-abundance performance depends on scenario and generator.** **a** Mean F1 for ALDEx2, ANCOM-BC2, LinDA and MaAsLin2 across five frozen scenarios under generator 1. **b** Mean per-dataset false-discovery proportion (FDP) for the same cells. **c** Joint FDP-power relationship in the strong sparse-effect scenario, with 20 targets among 120 tested features and absolute log2 effect 1.00. Marker color denotes method, shape denotes generator and area is proportional to F1. **d** LinDA FDP in primary seeds 4000-4009 and extension seeds 4010-4019. The no-effect control produced no discoveries. FDP is the mean of finite per-dataset values among datasets with at least one discovery; pooled FDP and bootstrap intervals are in the source data. Dashed lines mark FDP = 0.10.

**Fig. 3 | Core-feature recovery is strong until sequencing depth is confounded with occupancy.** **a** Mean core F1 across five core scenarios and 20 datasets per method-scenario cell. **b** Mean F1 across four non-null scenarios with 95% bootstrap intervals from 5,000 resamples of per-dataset metrics. **c** Mean false-core rate, with color capped at 0.10. **d** Recall-precision boundary under depth confounding. The null scenario has no true core features, so the false-core rate is the primary null endpoint. Project-balanced prevalence mirrors the project-level target definition and its advantage is partly definitional.

**Fig. 4 | Network inference and sequence classification expose different reliability boundaries.** **a** Mean synthetic direct-edge AUPRC across non-null validation scenarios; error bars show one standard deviation across five validation seeds. **b** Stability on three disjoint real-data panels, measured by top-decile edge Jaccard and degree-rank Spearman correlation. **c** Closed-set genus accuracy and macro F1 over five split seeds. **d** Unseen-genus rejection versus known-taxon retention; the threshold retains 95% of known validation queries. The Transformer was trained from scratch and is not a pretrained DNA-BERT model. Network edges are statistical associations, not ecological interactions.

**Fig. 5 | Project-panel holdout reveals limited structural transportability.** **a** Number of eight structural summaries meeting tolerances in three disjoint 15-project panels for the two generators. **b** Fixed-tolerance checks across 30 held-out panels from 10 repeated stratified three-fold partitions. **c** Empirical interval coverage for aggregate structural summaries from five predictive draws per fold. **d** Evidence decision separating within-panel calibration from structural transportability and universal ranking. The 30 panels are correlated because each project is held out once per partition. This analysis evaluates generator structure, not DA accuracy, FDP or ranking on real held-out projects.

**Fig. 6 | Project-held-out TARE-DA improves conditional prediction and supports conservative abstention.** **a** Utility MAE for TARE-DA and the development-only method-by-scenario prior across 10 repeated project-holdout partitions in the strong-effect scenario. **b** Method-level strong-effect MAE with one standard deviation across the 10 partitions. **c** Empirical coverage of the 95% conformal intervals. The dashed line marks nominal coverage 0.95. **d** ALLOW, CONDITIONAL and DENY decisions across 3,600 internal predictions. Strong-effect results received no ALLOW decisions under the frozen reliability criterion. TARE-DA predicts utility under simulator-defined effects and does not estimate biological truth in unlabelled projects.
