## Table 5 | Truth definitions, method targets and evaluation boundaries

| Module | Ground truth | Method target | Primary metrics | Evaluation boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Differential abundance | Target status and expected signed log2 effect after compositional renormalization | Feature-level condition contrast, with project blocking or random effects where supported | FDP, power, F1, direction accuracy | Absolute abundance is unobserved; closure effects are retained in the truth file |
| Core identification | Latent detection probability >=0.90 in >=80% of projects | Observed prevalence, project-balanced prevalence, or model-estimated occupancy | False-core rate, recall, F1, Brier score | Core is definition-dependent; detection failure is not absence |
| Network inference | Non-zero off-diagonal entries of a Gaussian precision matrix | Marginal CLR association or conditional partial association | Edge AUPRC, AUROC, hub-degree Spearman | Statistical edges are not ecological interactions |
| Sequence classification | SILVA genus label and held-out unseen-genus status | Supervised sequence classification and confidence-based rejection | Accuracy, macro F1, unseen-genus rejection, known-taxon retention | Complementary reference-set benchmark; not the same eight-summary simulation |

The four modules use different estimands. They form a shared risk map rather than a single leaderboard, and the classification benchmark is a complementary reference-set analysis rather than an eight-summary simulation module.
