# Figure legends

**Fig. 1 | An empirically anchored multi-study rhizosphere 16S rRNA benchmark.**
**a** Benchmark architecture linking 45 public rhizosphere projects, eight-summary parameter calibration, four analysis domains and repeated project-panel structural validation. **b** Samples per project (n = 45 projects; maize, rice and wheat, 15 projects each). Bars are ordered by crop. **c** Number of independently generated datasets, validation seeds or split seeds used in each benchmark. **d** Fraction of prespecified structural or null checks meeting operational tolerances. “Three-panel structure” summarizes six panel-by-generator combinations with three calibration seeds each; “held-out structure” distinguishes within-panel calibration from structural transportability analysis. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.

**Fig. 2 | Differential-abundance performance depends on scenario and generator.**
**a** Mean F1 for four differential-abundance methods across five frozen scenarios under generator 1. **b** Mean per-dataset realized false-discovery proportion (FDP) for the same method-by-scenario cells. **c** Joint FDP-power relationship in the strong sparse-effect scenario (20 target features among 120 tested features; absolute log2 effect size 1.00). Marker color denotes method, marker shape denotes generator, and marker area is proportional to mean F1. **d** LinDA realized FDP in primary seeds 4000–4009 and confirmation seeds 4010–4019; box centers show medians, boxes span the interquartile range, whiskers extend to 1.5 times the interquartile range, and points show individual seeds. Generator 1 contributes 20 seeds per scenario; generator 2 contributes five seeds and is a correlated perturbation of the same template. Dashed lines mark FDP = 0.10. FDP is the mean of finite per-dataset realized values among datasets with at least one discovery and is undefined when no discoveries are made; pooled FDP and bootstrap intervals are in Supplementary Table S3. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.

**Fig. 3 | Core-feature recovery is strong until sequencing depth is confounded with occupancy.**
**a** Mean core F1 across five frozen core scenarios and 20 datasets per method-by-scenario cell. **b** Mean F1 across the four non-null scenarios with 95% bootstrap confidence intervals from 5,000 resamples of finite per-dataset method metrics (n = 78–80). **c** Mean false-core rate; color scaling is capped at 0.10. **d** Recall-precision boundary in the depth-confounded scenario; points are method means over 20 datasets. The null scenario has no true core features, so F1, recall and precision are undefined and the false-core rate is the primary null endpoint. Project-balanced prevalence was calibrated after real-data exploratory work, and its advantage is therefore partly definitional. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.

**Fig. 4 | Network truth recovery and sequence classification expose distinct performance boundaries.**
**a** Mean synthetic direct-edge AUPRC across non-null validation scenarios; error bars are one standard deviation across five validation seeds for each scenario-method cell. **b** Cross-panel stability on three disjoint real-data panels, measured as top-decile edge Jaccard overlap and degree-rank Spearman correlation across three pairwise panel comparisons. **c** Closed-set genus accuracy and macro F1 over five independent reference-split seeds; error bars are one standard deviation across seeds. **d** Unseen-genus rejection versus retention of known taxa; horizontal and vertical error bars are one standard deviation across the five split seeds. The rejection threshold was calibrated to retain 95% of known validation queries. The k-mer Transformer was trained from scratch and is not a pretrained DNA-BERT model. Edges are statistical co-occurrence estimates and are not interpreted as ecological interactions. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.

**Fig. 5 | Repeated project-panel holdout reveals limited structural transportability.**
**a** Number of eight structural summaries meeting operational tolerances in three disjoint 15-project panels, separately for template and correlated-perturbation generators; bars are means over three calibration seeds per panel-generator combination. **b** Fixed-tolerance checks met across 30 held-out panels from 10 repeated stratified three-fold partitions. Boxes show the interquartile range, black lines show medians, whiskers extend to 1.5 times the interquartile range, and points show individual held-out panels. The 30 panels are correlated because every project is held out once in each partition and therefore 10 times per generator. **c** Empirical interval coverage for nine aggregate structural summaries. Each cell summarizes 30 held-out folds; intervals were obtained from only five predictive draws per fold, so values are coarse diagnostics rather than precise estimates of a nominal 95% interval. **d** Evidence decision separating internal execution and three-panel structural calibration from structural transportability and universal ranking. This analysis evaluates generator-level structure rather than method accuracy on real held-out projects. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.
